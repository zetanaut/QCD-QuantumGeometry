## Modular gauge and nuclear construction

The [modular architecture study](../evidence/quantum_geometric_modular_architecture.md) executes several distinct controls:

- **Non-Abelian information:** separate loop traces agree to numerical precision while a loop-commutator trace differs by 0.02032736. Keeping each loop's scalar trace is not an information-complete substitute for ordered transport.
- **Linked responses:** all 56 link/generator derivatives in the graph control are compared with independent perturbations. Dropping an edge response violates the applicable local classical identity.
- **Fermionic composition:** proton/neutron interpolators and spin couplers are exercised for A=1 and selected A=2,3,4 examples. An interpolator with valid labels is not a solved nucleus.
- **Composite overlap:** same-orbital proton/neutron products expose a norm of $10/9$, with a two-ordering Gram spectrum $\{0,20/9\}$. One common map transports normalization and observables.
- **Temporal positivity:** an unrestricted positive-entry neural kernel develops negative eigenvalues; a sufficient positive-character family passes, and the same neural function can still be used as a finite spatial potential with a positive transfer kernel.

These results locate useful learnable freedom around physical representations and transport. They do not establish the quantum local Gauss constraint, a continuum regulator, or nuclear binding.

## Latest constructive candidate

The [stable connection/chiral/sea study](../evidence/stable_connection_sea.md) provides a working exploratory effective-action class with a bounded positive coefficient of full $F[A]^2$ and a coercive chiral potential. Its finite noncommuting connection-dependent two-flavor determinant is evaluated directly.

The reference coupled slice has $\sigma=1.0011529223$ and $\theta=-0.5577742790$; omitting the sea changes the slice to $\sigma=0$ and $\theta=-0.5598682269$. This demonstrates sea backreaction within the finite reference. Only two coordinates were minimized, not an entire gauge-field vacuum.

Common mass/link/bilocal sources and exact retained-fermion determinant splitting are tested. Dropping induced contacts changes the linked response, and adding the full sea beside retained loops double counts. An independent Hermitian operator route handles arbitrary real pion textures and supplies a finite-Fock lower-bound control.

The next physical qualification concerns collective-field identity/measure, local Gauss law, canonical LF embedding, spatial variation, chiral/anomaly/regulator requirements, and common source matching. The algebraic cube is not an adopted physical lattice regulator. Physical binding, confinement, a matched quantum vacuum, and generated nucleon predictions remain unestablished.

## How to interpret the recorded evidence

The linked notes preserve direct derivations, independent positive controls, negative controls, numerical conditions, and reproduction entry points. Historical test counts describe the runs recorded in those notes. This documentation release does not convert collection counts into executed test passes or claim a fresh full-repository regression.

The most reusable outcomes are often restrictions revealed by failure: energy is insufficient to certify currents; conservation leaves transverse freedom; a state-only metric can miss source information; overlapping paths require a common inner product; and a geometry label cannot replace a canonical interaction node.
