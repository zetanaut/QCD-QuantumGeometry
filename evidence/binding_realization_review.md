## A concrete interaction-level possibility, with its risk exposed

One possible research direction is a matched gluonic correlation field,
so geometry is generated by gauge-field energy rather than assigned to
independent trees. An illustrative local action would contain

\[
 \mathcal L=\bar q(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu-m)q
 -\frac14\kappa(\chi)F^a_{\mu\nu}F_a^{\mu\nu}
 +\frac12\partial_\mu\chi\partial^\mu\chi-V(\chi)
 +\mathcal L_{\rm other\ retained/residual}.
\]

F is the **full SU(3)** field strength, with all eight gauge components and
their commutators. Chi is an illustrative color/flavor/chiral singlet, not
an identified extra QCD particle. Its gluonic/composite meaning, measure and
full-minus-retained matching would have to be established. No fitted
two-O(4) multiplet is silently relabelled as this field. The canonical term
is changed by -(kappa-1)F^2/4, so its normalization and coupling require
matching, not a dimension-only identification. Extra residual operators,
chiral dynamics and source partners are not assumed zero.

This is a known dielectric-type mechanism, not a claim of original physics.
[Martens et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0407215) investigate static
meson/baryon flux in an Abelian color reduction, calibrated to lattice
string information. Their fields, truncation, parameters and resulting
potentials are not adopted. A full-SU(3), source-matched quantum extension
would be a new obligation, not something that paper or this sketch has
already supplied for our project.

For kappa>0, ordinary-time canonical variables give, with metric +---,

\[
 \Pi_i^a=\kappa E_i^a,\quad (D_i\Pi_i)^a=\rho_q^a,
 \qquad\mathcal H_{g\chi}=
 \frac{\Pi^2}{2\kappa}+\frac{\kappa B^2}{2}
 +\frac{p_\chi^2}{2}+\frac{(\nabla\chi)^2}{2}+V(\chi).
\]

The quark energy and Gauss constraint complete the Hamiltonian. This
ordinary-time necessary check is not a derived interacting LF generator.
If chi is a chiral singlet, this term introduces no new explicit nonsinglet
chiral breaking, but also does not by itself prove dynamical chiral breaking,
a pion solution or a nucleon current. Probe dependence must come from the
same matched action and its state responses; a neutral extra field is not
an independent electromagnetic fitting head.

## What the simplest static limit actually says

Use only as a transparent local example

\[
 \kappa(\chi)=\varepsilon+\chi^2/f^2,\qquad
 V(\chi)=m_\chi^2\chi^2/2,\qquad
 \tau_0=m_\chi f>0.
\]

Chi, f and m_chi are GeV, epsilon dimensionless, tau0 GeV^2. No numbers are
selected. This is a dimension-six gauge/scalar interaction, not automatically
UV-matched QCD. Suppress magnetic/time/gradient energy only to obtain a
**lower bound**, not a full static solution. At fixed D=|Pi| and
x=chi^2/f^2>=0, independently minimizing the local energy gives

\[
 \min_{x\ge0}\left[\frac{D^2}{2(\varepsilon+x)}
                         +\frac{\tau_0^2 x}{2}\right]
 =\begin{cases}
 D^2/(2\varepsilon),&D\le\tau_0\varepsilon,\quad\varepsilon>0,\\
 \tau_0D-\tau_0^2\varepsilon/2,&D\ge\tau_0\varepsilon.
 \end{cases}
\]

The interior minimizer is x=D/tau0-epsilon; otherwise x=0. Both the energy
and its first derivative match at the boundary. At epsilon=0, the lower
bound is tau0|Pi|. In a commuting static color reference, Gauss law fixes
the flux Q through a sphere surrounding an isolated smooth charge. Then
integral_sphere |Pi| dS >= |Q|, and the energy between radii R1,R2 satisfies

\[
 E(R_1,R_2)\ge\tau_0|Q|(R_2-R_1).
\]

This shows how this **specified classical mechanism** can make isolated
flux cost diverge with distance. Tau0|Q| is a lower-bound coefficient, not a
calculated QCD string tension. The commuting reference does not replace
full non-Abelian quantum dynamics, light quarks, string breaking or a
physical p/n calculation. For a neutral separated cluster the net-flux
bound is zero; this does not prove cluster separability or nuclear forces.

For every epsilon>0, a constant-vacuum trial field instead has the finite
exterior energy

\[
 \chi=0,\quad\Pi_r=Q/(4\pi r^2),\qquad
 E(r>R)=\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\varepsilon R}.
\]

A smooth charge core makes the corresponding total trial energy finite.
It need not solve the scalar equation: an admissible finite-energy trial
already disproves infinite isolation cost **from this classical dielectric
mechanism** at a fixed positive floor. It does not disprove nonperturbative
confinement in full SU(3) QCD at positive gauge-kinetic normalization. The
local crossover radius is sqrt[|Q|/(4 pi tau0 epsilon)] for the radial
reference; sending the floor to zero is not a uniform long-distance limit.
Consequently a numerical floor is a physical change at large distance unless
its effect is controlled in the stated domain.

The minimizer, positive curvature, branch continuity/derivative and exterior
integral were independently checked with symbolic algebra. No flux-tube PDE,
quantum spectrum or nucleon fit was run, and no such validation is claimed.

## The unresolved quantum and matching choice

The classical confining limit just used has kappa(vacuum)=0. The quadratic
gluon kinetic coefficient around that vacuum vanishes. For epsilon>0,
canonical small-fluctuation normalization A_can=sqrt(epsilon) A changes the
coupling to g_eff=g_s/sqrt(epsilon), which is singular at fixed g_s as the
floor vanishes. This is not an innocuous correction to the perturbative
gluon vacuum used in the existing finite-order controls.

It does **not** prove that a self-adjoint singular-limit quantum theory or
representation on canonical variables is impossible. It means a quantum
Hamiltonian domain, nonperturbative vacuum, local Gauss-law realization,
retained/composite matching and LF/source map must be supplied and tested.
Keeping a positive floor supplies neither a proof that the zero-floor
mechanism survives nor an all-purpose QCD confinement approximation. A
scalar singlet also does not settle chiral dynamics or all missing channels.

That is the actual research fork. Investigating such gluonic vacuum dynamics
could make the project's geometric architecture physically more substantive,
but it is a new, substantial hypothesis and dependency. The current evidence
does not justify choosing this dielectric model, declaring it original QCD,
or predicting that its quantum completion will work. A different intended
confinement construction would lead to different state and matching work.
