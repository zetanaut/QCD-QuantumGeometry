## Action, approximation and information boundary

Take two real O(4) multiplets phi_i=(sigma_i,pi_i^a), with
Phi_i=sigma_i+i tau^a pi_i^a. At zero external fields,

\[
{\cal L}={\cal L}_{\rm QCD,canonical}(m)
+\frac12\sum_i\partial_\mu\phi_i\cdot\partial^\mu\phi_i
-\sum_i g_i\bar q(\sigma_i+i\gamma_5\tau^a\pi_i^a)q-V(\phi;m),
\]
\[
V=\sum_i{\lambda_i\over4}(\phi_i^2-a_i^2)^2
+{\kappa\over2}(\phi_1-\phi_2)^2-m\sum_i c_i\sigma_i.
\]

Fields, m and momenta are GeV; lambda and g are dimensionless; kappa,
c and the signed potential coefficients a_i^2 are GeV^2. A negative
a_i^2 is an allowed potential coefficient, not a negative physical pole
mass. Lambda_i>0 and kappa>0 make the bosonic potential bounded below.
Canonical positive kinetic terms and local interactions give the ordinary
tree causal principal part; the solved fluctuation masses are nonnegative.

The canonical term above specifies the intended action context. Its
quark/gluon implementation is preserved, not rederived or fully assembled
with all new collective interactions here. The numerical experiment solves
the bosonic classical vacuum with zero quark expectation, and probes the
resulting tree quark/source operators. It does **not** solve a quantum quark
gap equation or a composite qqbar bound pion. Its quark dressing is the
constant tree mass M=m+g.v, not a momentum-dependent QCD self-energy.
Its pion masses are curvature/propagator poles of the collective fields.
Their values are generated by this action after calibration, not predictions
independent of those calibration inputs.

The additional Yukawa/collective action is not matched to QCD. In
particular its quark exchange must not simply be added on top of already
retained QCD interactions as an authenticated residual. The exact full
spin/color canonical Born tensor is not replaced by a scalar/pseudoscalar
projection. At the generated tree mass, the preserved LF propagating plus
instantaneous quark exchange still agrees with the covariant amplitude.
The new Yukawa operator keeps Dirac x flavor x color order (24x24), with
all three isovector components and identity color transport. It is local
and color singlet, so it commutes with local SU(3); no new Wilson path is
invented for it. Existing nonlocal ordered gauge paths remain untouched.
This is portability, not full QCD matching or a proof of nuclear topology.

## Solved vacuum and responses from the same action

At phi_i=(v_i,0),

\[
H_\pi=\operatorname{diag}[\lambda_i(v_i^2-a_i^2)]
+\kappa\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
H_\sigma=H_\pi+\operatorname{diag}(2\lambda_i v_i^2),
\]
\[
H_\pi v=mc,\quad H_\pi e_n=m_n^2e_n,\quad e_n^Te_k=\delta_{nk},
\quad f_n=v^Te_n,\quad G_n=c^Te_n/2,
\quad f_n m_n^2=2mG_n.
\]

Pole phases are chosen consistently for the displayed f_n,G_n; this is the
tau^a/2 axial convention. In the broken chiral limit H_pi v=0 generates
a massless mode. The three parameter choices have chiral light eigenvalues
within 5.54e-17 GeV^2 of zero and positive remaining curvatures.

The aligned potential is strictly convex in x_i=v_i^2: the quartics are
strictly convex, while -sqrt(x1*x2) and -m c_i sqrt(x_i) are convex.
For positive sources, aligned positive fields minimize the orientation
terms. This identifies the unique positive radial vacuum; chiral orientation
degeneracy at m=0 is physical. The solver first minimizes this convex
potential, then refines the stationary equations. A direct gap-squared
minimization alone had stalled at the small-field boundary in a negative
initialization test; this was corrected, not accepted as a vacuum or hidden
by loosening the physical residual check.

For the light normalized pion mode e, the actual third potential derivative is

\[
(g_{\sigma\pi\pi})_i=2\lambda_i v_i e_i^2,
\qquad F_S(Q^2)=c^T(H_\sigma+Q^2I)^{-1}g_{\sigma\pi\pi},
\]
\[
F_S(0)={d m_\pi^2\over dm},\qquad
\langle r_S^2\rangle=6(\hbar c)^2
 {c^TH_\sigma^{-2}g_{\sigma\pi\pi}\over
  c^TH_\sigma^{-1}g_{\sigma\pi\pi}}.
\]

F_S has units GeV. The physical scalar-source convention is
O_S=-delta L/dm=qbar q-c.sigma. Eliminating the induced scalar fluctuation
produces the positive displayed pion matrix element. The quark scalar
vertex is 1+g^T(H_sigma+Q^2I)^-1c; at zero transfer it equals dM/dm.
Independent shifted-vacuum solves verify both mass derivatives. Actual
PyTorch second and third action derivatives reproduce H_sigma, all three
H_pi blocks and g_sigma-pi-pi. Scalar Feynman--Hellmann errors in the replay

The full two-mode quark source vertices, with nonsinglet tau/2 stripped

\[
R_\pi(t)=(H_\pi-tI)^{-1},\quad
\Gamma_A^\mu=\gamma^\mu\gamma_5+2q^\mu g^TR_\pi(t)v\gamma_5,
\quad 2m\Gamma_P=2m[1+g^TR_\pi(t)c]\gamma_5.

Using H_pi v=mc and M=m+g.v proves

For A=H_sigma-zI, arbitrary source endpoints l,r,

\[
l^TA^{-1}r={l_2r_2\over A_{22}}+
{(l_1-l_2A_{21}/A_{22})(r_1-A_{12}r_2/A_{22})

## Why more potential flexibility cannot fix this result

For a canonical constant kinetic metric, minimal gauging gives

\[
J^\mu_{nn}(p',p)=(p'+p)^\mu e_n^Te_n=(p'+p)^\mu,
\qquad F_V(t)=1,\qquad\langle r_V^2\rangle=0.
\]

An arbitrary local potential alters the masses and constant mixing, not
this diagonal tree vertex. A constant positive kinetic metric K likewise
gives e_n^T K e_n=1 after normalization. Adding more such multiplets or
replacing the potential by a flexible invariant DNN does not change the
argument. This is a limitation of the stated tree potential-only family,
not a theorem that all local field theories or quantum loop dynamics have
pointlike form factors. The computed zero is **not** a default assignment
to missing physical loops, vector channels or source operators.

**Reassessment:** do not promote or expand this potential-only candidate.
Preserve its common causal action, source algebra, successful checks and
failed withheld observable. Next test a transverse chirally/gauge-invariant
source interaction or its matched dynamical origin in the shared action.
It must supply source momentum dependence with linked one-/two-source
responses and standard retained subtraction, not an arbitrary F_V output.
Compare independent low-momentum scalar and vector responses, and identify
what a coefficient fit constrains versus what remains genuinely predictive.
Do not infer that a particular vector resonance, sigma-model loop scheme,
UV completion or production architecture is already selected.

The generic quark functional's causal completion, quantum dressing,
composite nucleon states, p/n covariance and modular nuclear/GTMD predictions
remain open. This result advances the architectural decision about necessary
source dynamics, not those completed-physics counts.
